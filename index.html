01 — The Problem

Anyone can share a link. Very few can deliver completion.

The bottleneck is not access to platforms. Every student already has the same links, the same resume, and the same lack of proof. The bottleneck is execution.

500–1,000
applications per entry-level IT role in India

application-to-offer conversion for freshers
80–90%
of resumes rejected by ATS before a human reads them
61%
of candidates never hear back at all

Government data proves the gap: the National Career Service portal posted 1.94 crore vacancies but recorded only 35.23 lakh actual placements. If a government portal cannot convert access into outcomes at scale, a forwarded link definitely will not.

04 — The Honesty Slide

We will never sell you a placement number. Here's why that protects you.

No honest partner can guarantee a job — not even a full degree does. We guarantee the things we control, report them monthly, and let the results speak.

We guarantee in writing
  • Every eligible student completes a live, ATS-optimised profile on NCS, AICTE portal and at least one private platform
  • A minimum of 3–5 verified interview opportunities per eligible student (drives, walk-ins, job fairs, challenges)
  • 2 documented mock interviews per student with written feedback
  • A QR-verified internship completion certificate
  • A monthly readiness report you can show parents and management
We do not guarantee
  • A job offer
  • A specific number of final selections
  • A specific salary package
  • A named employer on the certificate
  • A placement percentage

Student data & consent. Interview Champ runs entirely in the student's browser — answers and results never leave the device. Student data used for any HR-outreach module is processed only on the institute's written authorisation. The institute remains the data owner; Corporate Ready acts solely as a data processor for the duration of the engagement.
